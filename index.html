Agency Membership, Security & Investment Agreement

1. Data Security, Confidentiality & Anti-Intrusion

  • All personal data, client credentials, internal architecture, proprietary tools, and business records submitted or accessed through the agency are strictly confidential and must never be shared with unauthorized third parties.
  • Members must enforce strong authentication, endpoint protection, and secure handling practices to prevent unauthorized access, data leaks, or cyber intrusions.
  • Any unauthorized scanning, exploitation, tampering, or malicious activity against agency systems, client assets, or fellow members is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate termination and legal action.

2. Investment Lock-In Period & Early Withdrawal Rules

  • Financial investments made into the agency require a fixed lock-in commitment period (such as 3 years or 5 years, as mutually agreed upon at the time of investment).
  • If a member requests to break the agreement or withdraw capital before the lock-in period ends, the principal investment is not automatically withdrawable on demand.
  • Any early or special withdrawal requires formal review and approval from the responsible management/leadership, active members, and existing shareholders.

3. Dual Contribution Model (Capital & Skill) & Active Participation

  • Capital Track: Members who invest funds earn returns proportional to their verified capital contribution and agreed terms.
  • Skill Track: Members who invest skills have their contribution valued based on verified deliverables, technical execution, and agency impact.
  • No Passive Free-Riding: Passive inactivity without corresponding capital investment or active work output does not entitle a member to unearned income. Revenue and rewards align with actual capital invested and work/value delivered.
